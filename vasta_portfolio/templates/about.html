The studio

About us

VAST is an architecture and interior design studio in Bangalore.

We work across homes, workplaces and spaces for gathering. Each project begins with the people who will use it, the site it belongs to and the practical realities of building it.

Our process is collaborative and direct. We stay involved from the first conversation through construction, paying close attention to proportion, material and the details that shape everyday use.

We are interested in work that feels clear, useful and particular to its setting. That means listening carefully, testing ideas and making decisions that hold up beyond the first impression.
